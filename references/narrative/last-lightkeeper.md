# The Last Lightkeeper — short playable foundation

Avel is a small island where the lighthouse once helped the sea answer the stars. Its tide has stopped below an old causeway. Mara holds a fading tide lantern whose star pulses with three lights reflected beneath the water. The first stretch of the path is exposed, and the lighthouse lens is dark. This is the completed opening; continuations begin after it.

The scope is one shore, one keeper and one immediate magical problem. Quiet magic has observable causes: reflected light, lenses and tidal movement. Avoid unrelated prophecies, wars or an unseen rescuer. Mara is practical and curious; she wants to protect the shore while understanding its rhythm.
