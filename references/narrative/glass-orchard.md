# The Glass Orchard — short playable foundation

At dusk in Sere, a desert city's copper-framed glass orchard has stopped receiving water. Ivo carries a seed compass that responds to living roots. Its coral center points toward a sealed irrigation hatch. One coral-leaved sapling remains alive beneath the warm canopy. This opening is complete history; generated continuations must act after it.

The three initial actions are opening the sealed hatch, following the compass beneath the trees, and shading the last sapling. Each must reveal useful evidence or change the immediate situation. The second decision follows that consequence; the final chapter settles the water problem or the deliberate preservation of the last sapling. No third quest, unforeseen rescuer or invented water source should replace the reader's chosen action.

This story uses the same strict two-panel/two-decision engine. Ivo's identity, the compass's name, ownership, object changes, causal references and chronology obey shared validation. The environment's mechanisms can be discovered through visible actions; no magic is allowed to silently reset damage. Semantic story quality remains a separate assessment from schema and state validation.

All opening text and artwork are original authored material. Reference PNGs are rasterizations of local SVG geometry. There were no paid generation calls for these assets and no live OpenAI performance or quality result is claimed for this new world. Playability requires the mocked complete-journey acceptance gate.
