Original Lunatoon character; no likeness or third-party character reference.

## Identity anchors

| Feature | Fixed constraint |
| --- | --- |
| Age and build | 32-year-old adult man; lean adult proportions |
| Face | Angular jaw, straight nose, clean-shaven; stable face proportions |
| Skin | Warm brown, base `#a67450`; preserve tone under cyan light |
| Hair | Short black hair swept back; no alternate cut or color |
| Left eye | Small amber cybernetic eye on **his anatomical left**, viewer-right when frontal |

| Clothing | Mustard-yellow trench coat `#d2a751`, angular lapels and raised collar; charcoal shirt, dark trousers and boots |
| Prop | Palm-sized black device with cyan core; do not turn it into a phone or firearm |

Camera changes are allowed. Face, coat, eye side and body proportions are not. No hat, beard, oversized armor, costume swap, mirrored cybernetic eye, or photorealistic recasting. Lighting may affect shadow shapes, not the underlying identity palette.

## Performance and story role

Kael is an observant detective, guarded rather than invulnerable. He wants to identify who sent the signal and regain control of the encounter. His immediate difficulty is that the device knows him while the city's surveillance has gone silent. His reflex is to distrust machines; this can conflict with the need to examine evidence before deciding what it means. These are continuity constraints for this scene, not new lore or a multi-node character arc.

Expressions should be readable through brow, mouth and posture: suspicion, alarm, focused resolve. A close-up must show an emotional change caused by the first panel. His brief dialogue should sound like a detective thinking under pressure, not an omniscient exposition narrator.
