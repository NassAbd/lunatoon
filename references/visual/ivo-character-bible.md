# Ivo — The Glass Orchard

Ivo is a 27-year-old gardener with warm medium-tan skin, an oval face, dark-brown eyes, close-cropped dark-brown hair and one curled forelock. He wears round copper-rimmed glasses, no beard, a cream utility vest with two square pockets over a deep-plum tunic, rust-red trousers and ochre ankle boots. Preserve adult proportions, glasses, forelock, face and costume color blocks. His initial vest and tunic are dusted with terracotta soil.

The seed compass is a palm-sized copper disk with an ivory face, four plum petal-shaped pointer marks and a coral glass center. It begins active and held by Ivo. The large irrigation wheel remains fixed to its hatch; it must not become a second handheld compass.

Use geometric copper glasshouse arches, terracotta soil, plum dusk and sparse coral leaves. Flat shapes, plum-umber contours and broad two-tone shadows distinguish this solarpunk world from Neon Protocol and Avel. No ocean, cybernetic eye, mustard coat, seafoam cape or baked-in lettering. Dialogue remains an app overlay.

## Original asset provenance

`public/glass-orchard.svg`, `orchard-ivo.svg`, `orchard-compass.svg` and `orchard-hatch.svg` are original repository-authored vectors. `ivo-reference.svg` reuses the exact `g#ivo` geometry from the character opening for full-body, face and compass views. `ivo-reference.png` is a local Sharp rasterization of that SVG at 1500×900. No image generation, stock imagery or third-party artwork was used.

The reference and template version are frozen in each run; a future design revision must not overwrite saved references. Uploaded creator artwork is separate from these public authored assets.

The six POC 08 preview covers are also original vectors: `preview-velvet-signal.svg`, `preview-cloudbreak-express.svg`, `preview-quiet-giant.svg`, `preview-salt-stardust.svg`, `preview-paper-kingdom.svg` and `preview-blue-hour.svg`. Their story concepts are discovery-only, with no playable template or claimed generated chapters.
