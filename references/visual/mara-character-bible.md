# Mara — The Last Lightkeeper

Mara is a 24-year-old lighthouse keeper with warm deep-brown skin, an oval face, broad dark eyebrows, dark-brown eyes and short rounded black curls. Her small ivory shell hairpin is above her anatomical left ear (viewer-right from the front). She wears a seafoam cape with a cream collar, cream knit top, muted peach scarf, dark-green trousers and brown ankle boots. Preserve adult proportions and the same face, hairstyle, hairpin side and silhouette. The initial cape hem is damp from sea mist; later appearance changes come only from saved story state.

Her tide lantern is hand-sized and hexagonal, with brass edges, sea-glass panes, a narrow arched handle and one suspended golden star. Mara holds it at the opening. It begins active but faint; the lighthouse's fixed lens is dark. Changes to the lantern's condition or owner must be reflected in both the state records and the illustrations.

Use flat cream, seafoam, peach and muted green shapes with delicate dark-green ink and two-tone shadows. Light gold belongs to the lantern and star reflections. Keep the sea spacious, weathered stone readable and silhouettes uncluttered. No neon circuitry, yellow trench coat, cybernetic eye, photographic texture or baked-in lettering. Dialogue stays in application overlays.

## Original assets and provenance

All artwork is original repository-authored SVG geometry. No WEBTOON artwork, stock images or paid generation were used.

- `public/last-lightkeeper.svg`: existing original coastal cover, also used as the first opening beat.
- `public/lightkeeper-mara.svg`: original full figure and lantern beside the shore.
- `public/lightkeeper-lantern.svg`: original closer view of the held lantern and reflected lights.
- `public/lightkeeper-causeway.svg`: original landscape composition with the exposed path.
- `public/mara-reference.svg` and `.png`: full figure, face and lantern crops using the **same** `g#mara` geometry from `lightkeeper-mara.svg`. The PNG is a local Sharp rasterization at 1500×900, not an AI generation. No text labels are embedded in the artwork.

The reference sheet and template version are frozen into each new run's local asset records. New template versions must not replace the reference bytes belonging to existing runs. The code-native source can be edited for a later version, but this must be an intentional versioned change.
