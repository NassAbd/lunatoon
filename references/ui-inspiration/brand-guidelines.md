# Lunatoon brand — POC 06

An original small moon and spark accompany the lowercase **lunatoon.** wordmark. The platform voice is quiet, inviting and direct: “A little moonlight. A different story.” Story art supplies genre and drama; the surrounding interface gives it room.

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Canvas | `#FAFAF7` |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` |
| Primary text | `#202124` |
| Lunar-lime accent | `#D9F77C` |
| Border | `#E8E9E5` |

Use editorial serif headings with highly legible system sans-serif body text. Avoid downloading fonts or adding a typography dependency. Actions have clear focus states and restrained transitions. Respect reduced-motion settings.

Neon Protocol keeps its own midnight ink, blue slate, mustard coat and cyan device. Platform lime is an interface accent, not a replacement for the comic's palette. The upcoming coastal-fantasy concept uses seafoam, cream and sunset colors to distinguish genre; its availability must always say **Coming soon** or **Preview**.

All shipped iconography, opening compositions and cover treatments are original repository-authored vectors or existing original Lunatoon assets. No WEBTOON art, logos, cover images, screenshots or characters are distributed. Private generated images remain in ignored local storage, never as public catalogue assets.

Do not imply popularity, ratings, creator accounts, a playable preview, public sharing or cloud synchronization. The catalogue is small by design and saved reading is local only.
